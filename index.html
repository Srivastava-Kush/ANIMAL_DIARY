Inspiration

Our mission is to showcase the incredible diversity of wildlife around the world and raise awareness about conservation efforts. Every animal has a story, and every habitat matters.
When I was in 3rd grade I used to watch a lot of wildlife documentaries on TV and I was fascinated by the variety of animals and their behaviors.
I used to jot down the names of animals I found interesting in my notebook. This curiosity led me to explore more about wildlife and conservation as I grew older.
This project is a reflection of that childhood fascination and my desire to share the wonders of the wildlife.
I used to River Monsters, Deadly 60, Steve Irwin And similar tv shows.
